## About

I am a second-year B.Sc. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at **United International University (UIU), Bangladesh**, with a Diploma in Electrical Technology from **Gopalganj Polytechnic Institute** (CGPA 3.77/4.00). I enjoy turning circuit theory and numerical methods into working hardware and clean simulations.

- 🔬 **Researcher** at **CENTeR** (Center for Emerging Networks and Technologies Research), UIU
- 🤖 **Electrical sub-team member**, **UIU Mars Rover Team**, Best Asian Team at URC 2025
- 📡 Building embedded and IoT systems on ESP32 (ESP-NOW, Wi-Fi) and Arduino
- 📐 Implementing numerical methods and circuit analysis in MATLAB/Simulink

## Research Interests

| Area | Focus |
