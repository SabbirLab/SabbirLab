## About

I am a third-year B.Sc. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at **United International University (UIU), Bangladesh**, with a Diploma in Electrical Technology from **Gopalganj Polytechnic Institute** (CGPA 3.77/4.00). I like building complete systems: hardware, firmware, signal processing, and analysis.

- 🔬 **Researcher** at **CENTeR** (Center for Emerging Networks and Technologies Research), UIU
- 🤖 **Electrical sub-team member**, **UIU Mars Rover Team**, Best Asian Team at URC 2025
- 📡 Embedded and IoT systems on ESP32 (ESP-NOW, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth) and Arduino
- 📐 Numerical methods, DSP, and circuit analysis in MATLAB/Simulink
